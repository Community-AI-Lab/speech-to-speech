# Contributing to the speech-to-speech model

This model is at the research stage. We have not selected an architecture or collected a dataset.

## Start with an issue

1. Pick an [open issue](../../issues), or propose a new one with a specific deliverable.
2. Post a short plan on the issue.
3. Submit code, benchmark scripts, or a research note through a pull request.
4. Include installation and reproduction instructions, test results, and the licence/source of anything reused.

We prefer measurable results over general suggestions.

## Working with audio

- Use only properly licensed, appropriately consented audio.
- Don't upload confidential material, personal recordings without permission or employer-owned datasets.
- Document dataset sources and what use they permit, including commercial training if relevant.
- Avoid using unlicensed voices or cloning identifiable speakers without consent.

## Models and compute

Document GPU type, runtime, settings, evaluation method and versioned model/dataset references when you report results. Don't publish secrets or access tokens.

## Credits and commercial use

Contributor credit and revenue-sharing terms have not yet been agreed. Any early participation log is provisional; it does not create a guarantee of payment, royalties or equity. A formal agreement is required before commercial use of contributed IP.
